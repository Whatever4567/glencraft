# FP baked hands (1276)

Static fist, open-palm and cup meshes posed offline from the CC0 MakeHuman
topology in `scottish-base-v2` (`visual_arm_slot0_hand`, primitive 1) with
the `HeadSkin_CC0_toned` atlas (cropped to the hand UV island).

Linen: a lower-frequency derivative of `Scottish_Linen_eee6d6` used on the
FP sleeve to avoid stripe moiré.

These are viewmodel-only; the third-person Scottish rig is unchanged.
